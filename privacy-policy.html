Privacy Policy — Akash Player

Last updated: 24 November 2026
Developer: Akash Gazi
Contact: mdakashgaziofficial@gmail.com

Summary

Akash Player is a video player for the videos stored on your own phone. The app does not collect, store, or send your personal information to us or to any server. Your videos never leave your phone.

Information we collect

None. Akash Player has no user account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Your videos

The app reads the video files already stored on your device so it can list them by folder and play them. This reading happens only on your device. No video file, file name, thumbnail, or viewing history is uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

Permissions used

  • Videos / media (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): to find and play the videos stored on your phone.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): only when you tap the microphone button for voice search. Voice search uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most phones this is Google's speech recognition), which may send the spoken audio to Google to convert it to text and may need an internet connection. We do not receive, store, or see that audio or text.
  • Internet (INTERNET): used only by the phone's built-in speech recognition for voice search. The app itself does not download or upload anything.

Deleting videos

When you delete a video or a folder inside Akash Player, the file is deleted from your phone's storage using the standard Android media system. Deleted files cannot be recovered by the app. The app always asks you to confirm before deleting anything.

Children

Akash Player does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
